Route Information

🚆 Train Name

GORAKHDHAM EXPRESS

The Gorakhdham Express (Train Nos. 12555/12556) is a premier daily superfast train operated by the North Eastern Railway zone of Indian Railways, connecting Gorakhpur Junction in Uttar Pradesh to Bathinda Junction in Punjab over a distance of roughly 1,123 kilometers. Named after the revered saint Guru Gorakhnath, this ISO 9001:2015 certified train is renowned for its reliable punctuality, cleanliness, and security, utilizing modern LHB rakes to ensure passenger comfort. Passing through major hubs like Lucknow, Kanpur, and New Delhi, it offers a full suite of travel accommodations ranging from AC First Class and AC Tier classes to Sleeper and General unreserved coaches, making it one of the most vital and heavily patronized long-distance links between eastern Uttar Pradesh and northwest India.

📍 Route

​

The travel corridor between Gorakhpur Junction (GKP) and New Delhi / Anand Vihar is one of the busiest and most vital rail links connecting Eastern Uttar Pradesh to the National Capital Region, spanning a distance of roughly 785 kilometers via major hubs like Basti, Gonda, Lucknow Charbagh, and Kanpur Central. ​Among the various train options available, the Gorakhdham Superfast Express (12555) is a prominent daily choice, departing Gorakhpur at 4:20 PM and arriving at New Delhi Railway Station at 5:15 AM the next morning, taking approximately 13 hours with comfortable LHB coaches across AC and Sleeper classes. ​For travelers looking for faster or alternative transit, the Gorakhpur Vande Bharat Express (22549) offers a much quicker daytime journey of just about 8 hours and 20 minutes, operating every day except Sunday and arriving at Anand Vihar Terminal in the afternoon.
