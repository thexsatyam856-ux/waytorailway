Route Information

🚆 Train Name

MAHARAJA EXPRESS

Train number commonly listed as 12587.Maharajas' Express ki "The Indian Splendour" journey Delhi se shuru hokar Agra, Ranthambore, Jaipur, Bikaner, Jodhpur aur Udaipur se hote hue Mumbai mein samapt hoti hai. Yeh duniya ki sabse behtareen luxury trains mein se ek hai, jo yatriyon ko Bharat ki shahi virasat, itihaas aur sanskriti ka anubhav karati hai. Is safar ke dauran mehmaan alag-alag aithihasik kilon, rashtriya udyanon aur sundar shehron ki sair karte hain, jisse ye yatra ek yaadgar anubhav ban jaati hai.

📍 Route

Maharajas' Express - The Indian Splendour Route (Delhi to Mumbai):
Day 1: Delhi — Safdarjung Railway Station se shuruat aurboard karne ke baad safar ki ravangi.
​ Day 2: Agra — Taj Mahal aur Agra Fort ke aithihasik darshan.
Day 3: Ranthambore & Jaipur — Ranthambore National Park mein jungle safari aur Jaipur mein Pink City ki sair (Amber Fort).
Day 4: Bikaner — Junagarh Fort aur regular sand dunes par anokha anubhav.
Day 5: Jodhpur — Mehrangarh Fort aur Mandore Garden ki sair.
Day 6: Udaipur — City Palace aur Lake Pichola ki boat ride.
Day 7: Mumbai — CSMT (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus) par yatra ka aant.
