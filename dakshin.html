Route Information

🚆 Train Name

DAKSHIN EXPRESS

Train Numbers: 12721 / 12722

Connects the Delhi region with Hyderabad via important stations in central and southern India.

📍 Route

H. Nizamuddin → Mathura → Agra → Gwalior → Jhansi → Bina → Bhopal → Itarsi → Nagpur → Balharshah → Kazipet → Secunderabad → Hyderabad Deccan.
