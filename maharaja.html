ROUTE INFORMATION

🚆 Train Name

MAHARAJAS' EXPRESS

Journey: The Indian Splendour

Duration: 6 Nights / 7 Days

This luxury train journey connects historic cities, royal palaces, cultural attractions and wildlife experiences. Guests travel from Delhi to Mumbai through Rajasthan and Agra.

📍 Journey Highlights

Day 1: Delhi to Agra — Taj Mahal.

Day 2: Agra Fort and Ranthambore safari.

Day 3: Jaipur and Amber Fort.

Day 4: Bikaner, Junagarh Fort and sand dunes.

Day 5: Jodhpur and Mehrangarh Fort.

Day 6: Udaipur, Lake Pichola and City Palace.

Day 7: Arrival in Mumbai.
